How we work

One team from first sketch to the last fixture aimed

Most suppliers sell you fittings. We take responsibility for how the space looks at night, so design, products and installation sit with one team.

01

Understand the space

A site visit and brief: how the space is used, what should be seen first, and the mood you want at each hour.

02

Lighting design

A layered concept of ambient, accent and task light, with fixture selection and placement, led by Suyash Narsaria.

03

DIALux simulation

Lux levels, uniformity and glare checked in DIALux reports before a single fixture is bought.

04

Supply & fabrication

Catalogue luminaires with CREE and Bridgelux chips, BIS certified, plus custom profiles and fittings built to your layout.

05

Installation

Our team installs, aims and commissions the lighting, and sets scenes and controls.

06

Service at your site

After handover a technician comes to your premises. You never have to send fixtures back.
