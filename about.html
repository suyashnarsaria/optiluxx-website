About Optiluxx

A lighting company that starts with design

Optiluxx Electricals LLP was founded in Kolkata in 2014. We are a complete lighting solutions provider: we design the lighting, prove it in simulation, supply or fabricate the luminaires, install them and service them on site.

Lighting design

Every project begins on paper, not in a catalogue

We plan light in three layers: ambient for comfort, accent for meaning, and task for clarity. We pick one primary colour temperature for a space and create variety through intensity and beam, not mixed whites.

Before you buy anything, we verify the scheme in DIALux simulation reports covering lux levels, uniformity and glare. Then we specify the exact fixture, beam and finish for each position.

When the catalogue does not have the right shape, we fabricate it: custom linear profiles, geometric fittings and bespoke luminaires built to your layout.

Founder & Lead Lighting Designer

Suyash Narsaria

Suyash qualified as a Chartered Accountant in 2014 after his articleship at Deloitte, Mumbai, and then chose a different path: light. He founded Optiluxx the same year and has led its design work ever since.

He personally designs Optiluxx’s schemes, from homes and hotels to heritage façades such as Khalsa College in Amritsar and the Diamond Heritage building on Strand Road, Kolkata. The financial discipline stays: clients get a design that is beautiful and a bill of materials that makes sense.
